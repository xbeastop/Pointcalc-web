Features

Everything you need to run a tournament.

Built for BGMI, PUBG Mobile and Free Fire organizers. Every section below is a feature you can use today — no spreadsheet, no Photoshop, no copy-pasting from screenshots.

★ AI Powered

Upload a screenshot. Get a finished table.

PointCalc's AI reads BGMI, PUBG Mobile and Free Fire lobby and result screens directly. Placements and kills are extracted automatically — and from that, PointCalc generates your full point table, top fraggers, MVPs and warhead stats. Review, edit, publish — all inside the app.

  • ✓Lobby screenshots → team names, slot order & player IDs
  • ✓Result screenshots → placements & kills per team
  • ✓Auto-derived: point table, top fraggers, MVPs, warheads
  • ✓Bulk match processing on the Premium + AI tier
  • ✓Manual override on any value — you stay in control
Point Tables

Live standings, published in seconds.

Generate a fresh point table after every match. Define your own rules — placement points, kill points, tiebreakers, bonus multipliers — and PointCalc keeps the math consistent across all your matches.

  • ✓Custom point system per tournament
  • ✓Daily and overall standings
  • ✓Tie-break by kills or position — your call
  • ✓Export-ready images in your brand colors
