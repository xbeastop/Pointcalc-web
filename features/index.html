Point Tables

Live standings, published in seconds.

Generate a fresh point table after every match. Define your own rules — placement points, kill points, tiebreakers, bonus multipliers — and PointCalc keeps the math consistent across all your matches.

  • ✓Custom point system per tournament
  • ✓Daily and overall standings
  • ✓Tie-break by kills or position — your call
  • ✓Export-ready images in your brand colors
Stat Cards

Warhead boards & top fraggers.

The kill leaderboards your audience screenshots. Warhead ranks teams by total kills; top fraggers ranks players into creator-grade MVP cards — all built straight from your match data, no extra uploads. Switch the character art between BGMI and Free Fire.

  • ✓Warhead — team kill leaderboard on a broadcast podium
  • ✓Top fraggers — your highest-killing players, ranked
  • ✓Auto-generated from the same match — zero extra work
  • ✓Game-specific character art and themes
Broadcast

Slot lists, posters & certificates.

Everything around the matches, too. Add teams, players and logos in-app and PointCalc lays out a broadcast-ready slot list automatically — plus a poster for every squad and winner certificates for your top finishers, all in your colors.

  • ✓A team poster for every squad in the event

Editing

Edit any match, anytime

Open a past match to fix a placement, kills or a single player's finishes — or remove a match's points entirely. Standings recalculate instantly across daily, overall and season tables.

Sharing

Share a tournament

Export a tournament as a file and hand it to a co-host, so the work can be split on long event days — they pick up exactly where you left off.

Registration

Team Cards

Skip the Google Form. Players fill their team and roster in the app and send you a file — open it in PointCalc and import it straight into any slot.

Seasons

Weekly & monthly stats

Merge several tournaments into one set of season standings with points combined across events — then flatten it into a standalone, fully editable table.

Safety

Backup & restore

Never lose an event. Automatic Google Drive backup on Android, and shareable backup files on iOS to move everything to a new phone.

Import

Your data & designs

Build a tournament from an Excel/CSV template, and import your own custom background designs to publish in your exact brand.

Scoring

Full scoring control
