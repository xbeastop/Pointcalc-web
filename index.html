Meet xbeastop

One developer, building the toolkit India's esports community actually needed.

I started PointCalc in September 2021. I was a PUBG player running scrims with friends, and the worst part of tournament day was never the matches — it was the after-math. Hours of manual point calculations, then hours more in Photoshop putting the result graphics together.

I was a BCA student who only knew C at the time. I watched a 14-hour Android tutorial, started building the day after, and had a working prototype inside a week. That's when it clicked: instead of one-off builds for each org, ship one app that any organizer can customize.

These days I'm an Android developer at Wrench Solutions in Kochi by day, and PointCalc is still my side project — now at 100K+ downloads, with AI screenshot extraction and an iOS build.
