Meet xbeastop

Built by one developer who used to run PUBG scrims.

I started PointCalc in September 2021. I was a PUBG player running scrims with friends, and the worst part of tournament day came after the matches: hours of working out points by hand, then more hours in Photoshop making the result graphics.

I was a BCA student who only knew C at the time. I watched a 14-hour Android tutorial, started building the day after, and had a working prototype inside a week. Then I had a better idea: instead of building something separate for each org, make one app that any organizer can set up their own way.

These days I'm an Android developer at Wrench Solutions in Kochi by day, and PointCalc is still my side project. It now has AI screenshot extraction, an iOS build, team registration links and PointCalc Studio for the designers in the community.

Teams sign up
from one link.

Share one link, and captains fill in their team and players from their browser, so you stop copying names out of WhatsApp. You check each team and approve it in the app.

  1. Share your link

    Pick Open registration when you create a tournament, add your WhatsApp number and match time, and publish.

  2. Captains sign up

    They open the link in any browser. They don't need the app or an account.

  3. You approve

    You get an alert for each new team. Check their proof, then approve, or reject with a reason they can fix.

  4. Make groups

    Approved teams fill the slots in order. Split them into groups, and each group becomes a tournament ready for points.

Also included

  • Entry tasks, like a follow on Instagram, an in-game ID or a payment screenshot
  • Drag teams between slots, or add a team yourself
  • Message every captain on WhatsApp with their slot already filled in
  • Your logo, prize pool, rules and colors on the page
  • Pause sign-ups, post an update or export the team list
