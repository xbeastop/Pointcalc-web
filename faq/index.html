Questions, answered.

What people ask us most about PointCalc: the games, the AI, team registration, pricing and more.

PointCalc is a tournament app for esports organizers running BGMI, PUBG Mobile and Free Fire events. Its AI reads your lobby and result screenshots, picks up placements and kills, and builds the points table, top fragger cards, MVP and warhead stats, team posters, slot lists and certificates. Teams can also sign up for your tournament from one link. It's on iOS and Android, with 150K+ downloads.

PointCalc supports BGMI (Battlegrounds Mobile India), PUBG Mobile and Free Fire. The AI reads lobby and result screens from all three games, and the top fragger, MVP and warhead stats come from that match data.

Upload the lobby and result screenshots from your match. PointCalc's AI reads the placements, kills and player stats off the images and fills in your points table. You can check and edit any value before you publish. On Premium + AI you can upload a whole match at once, and PointCalc puts the standings together.

Yes. Pick Open registration when you create a tournament, then share the link in your groups. Captains fill in their team, players and any entry tasks you set, like a follow on Instagram or a payment screenshot. You get an alert for each sign-up, check their proof and approve them in PointCalc. Approved teams fill your slots, and you can split them into groups when you're ready. Yes. Export a tournament as a file and share it with a co-host. They import it into PointCalc and carry on from where you left off, which helps when you split the work on a long event day.

Team Cards are the file-based way to add teams. A player fills in their team name and roster in the app and sends you the file on WhatsApp or Discord. You open it in PointCalc and import it into a tournament slot. Organisers also use Team Cards to split set-up work between staff.
